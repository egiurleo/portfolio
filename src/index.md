<p class="lede" markdown="1">👋 Hi, I'm Emily (she/her), an engineering manager helping build the next generation of Ruby developer tools.</p>

At [Shopify](https://shopify.engineering), I lead the Ruby Developer Experience team, where we build and maintain tools like [Sorbet](https://sorbet.org/), a gradual type checker for Ruby, and the [Ruby LSP](https://github.com/Shopify/ruby-lsp), the language server that powers autocomplete, go-to-definition, and other editor features for Ruby developers. Our goal is to make writing Ruby feel as fast, safe, and enjoyable as possible — for engineers at Shopify and for the wider Ruby community.

Outside of Shopify, I'm the co-founder and CEO of [WNB.rb](https://wnb-rb.dev), a community for women and non-binary Ruby developers. What started as a small meetup has grown into a thriving community with a monthly meetup, book club, interview study group, conference proposal review group, and more — all focused on helping a more diverse group of people make an impact in the Ruby community.
