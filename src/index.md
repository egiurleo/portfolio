<p class="lede" markdown="1">👋 I'm Emily, a software engineer building the next generation of Ruby developer tools.</p>

I currently manage the Ruby Developer Experience team at [Shopify](https://shopify.engineering), where our goal is to create an opinionated, state-of-the-art coding experience for Ruby developers (human or otherwise). We build and maintain tools like [Sorbet](https://sorbet.org/), the [Ruby LSP](https://github.com/Shopify/ruby-lsp), and [Rubydex](https://github.com/Shopify/rubydex).

I am also the co-founder and CEO of [WNB.rb](https://wnb-rb.dev), a community for women and non-binary Ruby developers. What started as a small meetup has grown into a thriving non-profit, all focused on helping a more diverse group of people make an impact in the Ruby community.
