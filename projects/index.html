Projects

Squeezr

Ongoing

Squeezr is an iOS app I designed and built to make it easier to keep up with daily pelvic floor exercises. It offers customizable routines, a guided timer with audio and haptic cues, daily reminders, session history tracking, and a companion Apple Watch app for discreet workouts on the go. Squeezr is free to use, and I'm currently working on bringing it to Google Play.

WNB.rb

Ongoing

I am the co-founder and CEO of WNB.rb, a virtual community for women and non-binary Ruby developers, started with the goal of supporting a more diverse group of people in making an impact on the Ruby community. It has grown into a thriving community of technologists, with initiatives including a monthly meetup, book club, interview study group, conference proposal review group, and more!

RubyConf Mini

November 2022

In the wake of the political changes making it unsafe for some community members to attend RubyConf 2022, I worked with Jemma Issroff and Andy Croll to organize an alternative option. RubyConf Mini took place in November 2022 in Providence, Rhode Island. It featured 30 sessions, with a heavy focus on inclusion and belonging.

FreeFrom Map and Scorecard

June 2021

FreeFrom is an organization that provides survivors of domestic violence with the tools they need to achieve financial independence. Working with other volunteers at Ragtag, I served as the backend tech lead for website that shows which states best support survivors' financial independence. Using this tool, activists can advocate for laws that help survivors recover from abuse.

FreeFrom Compensation Compass

November 2019

FreeFrom is an organization that provides survivors of domestic violence with the tools they need to achieve financial independence. Working with other volunteers at Ragtag, I served as the backend tech lead for a website that helps survivors acquire financial compensation for the harm they endured.
